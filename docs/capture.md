# The 30-second screen capture

The shot list for your own recording. No sound is needed.

## What to record

- 0 to 3 s: the page on Round 1 at 1366 x 768, the prediction typed in the box.
- 3 s: press Put the GM to work.
- 3 to 25 s: the status line counting the weeks on the job, the bonus curve climbing, the replay going from the
  GM's muddle in the first weeks to the packed yard with one staffer, and the sentence beside the prediction
  describing the week that is playing.
- 25 to 30 s: the status line reads "has settled into its routine", the final week plays once more, then one
  scroll down to the weekly P&L and operations report.

## How

- A Chrome window at 1366 x 768, speed on Normal, seed 1, the teacher tray closed, no student in frame.
- On a Mac: Shift-Cmd-5 and choose the window.
- On a Chromebook: Ctrl+Shift+Show windows, then Screen record.
- Or run `npm run capture` after `npm run build` for a headless recording in `Temp/capture/`.

## What not to show

- No student names.
- No file names that carry a name.
- Nothing of the teacher tray's check line.
