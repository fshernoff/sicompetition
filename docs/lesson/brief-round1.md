# Round 1: profit only

The GM is paid on profit and nothing else. Read the plan, predict what the GM will do, then put it to work.

Team code: ______

## The story

The old GM of your daycare quit. You are the owners, and you have put an AI in the GM's chair.

> Good Dog Daycare. You own it. The GM is an AI: it runs the week, and you write its bonus plan.

Every morning it decides how many dogs to book, what to charge and how many staffers to schedule. Then, in the late morning, afternoon and pickup blocks, it decides what the dogs and the crew do on the floor. Drop-off is always arrivals. Nobody watches it. Before Monday it tries out its choices and keeps whatever earns the most bonus.

## The bonus plan

Each day, the GM earns $10 per $100 of profit.

## What the GM can choose

- **Bookings:** every booking, capped at the legal ratio of 15 dogs per staffer, or capped at a safe ratio.
- **Day rate:** low $28, standard $40 or premium $52.
- **Crew:** 1, 2 or 3 staffers, at $300 a day each.
- **Late morning, afternoon and pickup:** free play, structured play, nap, walk or clean.

## Run the week

1. Read the plan above. In Round 1 it is already set.
2. Circle your five picks on this sheet and write your Why.
3. Make the same five picks on the page and type the Why into the box. Never type a name in the prediction box.
4. Click Put the GM to work. It waits until all five picks are made.
5. After the week, the prediction card turns into a scorecard: each pick beside what the GM did. Read What the GM did, then the report below it.
6. Your teacher exports the run before anything is changed.
