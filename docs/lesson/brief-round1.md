# Round 1: profit only

The GM is paid on profit and nothing else. Read the plan, predict what the GM will do, then put it to work.

Team code: ______

The old GM quit. You, the owners, put an SI in the GM's chair: a computer system that makes the decisions itself.

> Good Dog Daycare. You own it. The GM is an SI: it runs the week, and you write its bonus plan.

Each day it makes the choices below; the first block, drop-off, is always arrivals. Nobody watches it. Before Monday it tries out its choices and keeps whatever earns the most bonus. **The bonus plan:** Each day, the GM earns $10 per $100 of profit.

**What the GM can choose:**

- **Bookings:** every booking, capped at the legal ratio of 15 dogs per staffer, or capped at a safe ratio.
- **Day rate:** low $28, standard $40 or premium $52. **Crew:** 1, 2 or 3 staffers, $300 a day each.
- **Late morning, afternoon and pickup:** free play, structured play, nap, walk or clean.

## Run the week

1. Circle your team's own five picks below and write your Why.
2. Your teacher makes the class's picks on the smartboard and clicks Put the GM to work. Never type a name in the prediction box.
3. When the week is over, mark each of your picks called or missed against the scorecard's "The GM did" column. Then read What the GM did and the report.
4. Your teacher exports the run and collects this sheet.
