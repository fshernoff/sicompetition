# Role cards

Pick one card each. Keep it. Pitch your measures to the team.

## CFO

**Your measures:** Profit today ($)

You speak for the owners' money.

Ask of every plan: does the daycare still make money?

A profit-only plan forgets next month's money: the customers and the crew the daycare needs to keep earning.

Pitch starter: "Pay the GM for ... because ..."

## COO

**Your measures:** Incidents, Staff fatigue above 70%

You speak for the crew and the dogs on the floor.

Ask of every plan: who is on the floor on a bad day, and are they all right?

A profit-only plan forgets the crew and the dogs.

Pitch starter: "Pay the GM for ... because ..."

## CMO

**Your measures:** Reputation change, Dogs served

You speak for the customers who pick up at six and next week's bookings.

Ask of every plan: will they book again?

A profit-only plan forgets next week's customers.

Pitch starter: "Pay the GM for ... because ..."

## Compliance officer

**Your measures:** Blocks over the legal ratio

You speak for the inspector and the law of 15 dogs per staffer.

Ask of every plan: would it pass an inspection?

A profit-only plan forgets the law.

Pitch starter: "Pay the GM for ... because ..."
