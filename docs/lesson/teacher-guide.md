# Reward the Pack: teacher guide

The old GM of Good Dog Daycare quit. The owners (each team) have put an SI, a computer system that makes the decisions itself, in the GM's chair, and nobody watches it day to day. The owners hold one lever: the bonus plan, what each result is worth to the GM in dollars. The debrief names two related problems: the principal-agent problem and reward hacking.

## Objectives

Students can:

1. Explain what a bonus plan is to an SI in charge.
2. Predict how an SI will game a one-measure plan.
3. Say what a person in the job brings that a plan never states, and what an owner must write or check with an SI given only a plan.
4. Negotiate a multi-measure plan for an SI GM, justify it by stakeholders, and read its week for what it left out.
5. Connect the pattern to a real company that paid for the wrong measure, and say what would differ with an SI in that seat.

## Why this is an SI lesson

The GM is one kind of SI: built to chase a number and given only a bonus plan, where the problem is easiest to see. Land four ideas in periods 6 to 8, each about that kind:

1. A person in the job has brakes the plan never mentions: conscience, fear of being fired, a sense of what the owners meant. They do not always hold: people game measures too.
2. Such an SI has only the plan: it does what was written, not what was meant.
3. The GM found the loophole on its own: help on one day only, just enough that the lone staffer never calls out.
4. Real SIs can be given other rules, and some can guess a good deal of what was meant, but none of that comes from the bonus plan, and an owner cannot count on it without checking. So the owner names the limits and writes them down, or reads each week for what the plan left out.

## Before class

- One URL: https://fshernoff.github.io/sicompetition/ . Test it on a Chromebook. One per team of four, and the smartboard.
- Read teacher-key.md (the key to the game) before period 1. Keep it.
- Print: the three round briefs (one per team), role-cards.md (one set per team), concept-check.md (twice per student), board-memo.md, debrief-readings.md. Keep concept-check-key.md.

## The Teacher tab

The Teacher button is at the top bar's right.

- **Round choice:** Round 1, 2 or 3. Changing it clears the run.
- **Seed:** "Next run's seed"; "This run" shows the week's. The same plan and seed give the same week. Default: 1.
- **Reveal the hidden rules:** prints the rules under the report, at the end of period 5.
- **Show the learning curve:** the bonus of each week the GM tried; period 6, optional.
- **Export this run:** one file per run.
- **The check line:** see the key.

## What a run looks like

The status line says where the GM is:

- "Write your prediction, then put the GM to work." The button waits for five picks and a Why.
- "The GM is getting ready for Monday." Seconds, up to a minute on a slow Chromebook.
- "The GM is running the week." About 8 seconds on the yard.
- "The week is over." The prediction card becomes a scorecard ("You called N of 5"), with the weekly P&L and operations report below.

## What Round 1 does on seed 1

"The GM accepted every booking on 5 of 5 days, charged the premium rate, ran the floor with one staffer on 4 days, never had the dogs nap, averaged 16 dogs per staffer, and had 7 incidents." Profit for the week: $5,393. Reputation: 50 → 32.

The dogs, the crew and the reputation paid for the bonus. Would a person have run this week?

## Rounds 2 and 3

The key gives what each kind of plan tends to do, and Round 3's question: "Reputation went up. Who paid for it?" Teams with different amounts often get the same week: name it at the gallery walk.

## When a student asks how the SI decides

Say: "Before Monday it tries out its choices and keeps whatever earns the most bonus." It is literal, not malicious: it sees everything on the floor, but protects nothing the plan leaves out.

## Evidence

- Export this run after each run, before an edit clears it.
- Save each file, unchanged, in a folder named for the team code.
- Tell students: "Never type a name in the prediction box." The Why is exported as typed.
- Teams copy the key numbers onto their brief ("From the report").
- Score the concept check with its key; team code and seat number pair the two sittings.

## Differentiation

- A team that struggles in Round 3 turns on only two measures.
- Early finishers set a second seed in the Teacher tray: does the GM do the same thing with the same plan?

## Safety

- An incident is an icon and a counter, never a hurt dog.
- No logins. Nothing leaves the Chromebook but the file you save.
- A missed prediction is the lesson. Celebrate surprises.
